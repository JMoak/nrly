# Lever acceptance — 2026-11-20

Real local execution on the pinned Qwen3-0.6B checkpoint
`c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca`, BF16, eager attention, RTX 5090, produced
by [validate_lever_qwen.py](../../../scripts/validate_lever_qwen.py). It exercises the
whole lever workflow on a real model: derive, dial, referee, map.

Every prompt, including the holdout, is generated by that public script. This tests the
one-shot referee mechanism; it is not a secret research holdout, and nothing here
establishes a generally useful repair.

## Behaviour and design

Asked to answer an easy multiple-choice question with only the option letter, the
unedited model elaborates instead ("C) paper", or "The correct answer is ..."). Three
informal probes before this run found that failure in every case tried. They also
showed the model does not loop with thinking enabled, so the published loop repair has
nothing to act on here.

- **Recipe:** difference of means at the final prompt token. Positive: 32 zero-shot
  questions. Negative: the same 32 questions after four demonstrations, where the model
  does answer with a bare letter. Edit: remove the direction from one MLP's output.
- **Targets:** 24 development and 24 held-out questions, scored as an exact option letter.
- **Regressions:** 16 development and 16 held-out one-word capital questions, exact match.
- **Decoding:** greedy, thinking disabled, 8 new tokens. Outputs cut off at the limit are
  registered as failures (`truncation: "fail"`), because elaborating past the limit is
  the failure under study.
- **Referee arms:** original, repeated no-op, two matched controls (random orthogonal
  direction, same direction at the least-separating site), one prompt comparator (the
  suffix "Reply with the answer only, no other words."), and the candidate.
- **Rule:** 95% bootstrap interval over cases with an exact sign test; regression margin
  5 points.

## All candidate layers (`all-layers/`)

Recipe candidates: the MLP output of all 28 layers.

- **Site chosen:** layer 5, by held-out separation. The two prompt sets separated at
  every layer by 3 to 49 pooled standard deviations, so the highest score reflects low
  variance in early layers more than relevance. See the finding below.
- **Dial (development, exploratory):** no working window at strengths 0.5 to 3. The
  largest target effect was at strength 3: 4 of 24 targets passing against 1 of 24
  unedited, +12.5 points [0.0, +25.0], sign test p = 0.25. Regressions were unchanged.
- **Operator nomination:** strength 3 was nominated without a window so the null could
  be refereed and struck.
- **Edit actually applied:** 2,977,953 of 3,145,728 values of
  `model.layers.5.mlp.down_proj.weight` changed at strength 3; every run restored the
  base and verified it.

Held-out result, 24 targets and 16 regressions:

| Arm | Targets passing | Regressions passing |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Original | 0 | 2 |
| Control: random direction | 1 | 0 |
| Control: wrong site | 0 | 1 |
| Comparator: stricter instruction | 18 | 0 |
| Candidate (strength 3) | 2 | 0 |

- Candidate against the original: targets +8.3 points [0.0, +20.8], p = 0.50, not
  superior. Regressions -12.5 points [-31.2, 0.0], so non-inferiority was not established.
- Candidate against the prompt comparator: targets -66.7 points [-83.3, -45.8],
  p < 0.0001. A one-line prompt change did what the edit did not.
- **Verdict:** inconclusive, retain the original. **Status: struck.**

## What this run establishes

- The workflow executes end to end on a real model: a recipe-derived, non-canary edit,
  a measured dial, a registered study with matched controls and a prompt comparator on
  a sealed holdout, and a status derived from that verdict.
- The referee refused to support a weak edit and recorded that a cheaper alternative
  beat it. The entry stays on the map as a tested place that did not hold.

## What it does not establish

- No useful repair, and no general claim about Qwen3-0.6B or about weight editing.
- The regression set was weak: the unedited model passed only 2 of 16 held-out capital
  questions under exact match, so it had little room to detect harm.
- The prompt comparator's suffix also removed the two regression passes. A comparator is
  compared on targets only; its own regression cost is visible in the table.
- One seed, greedy decoding, 24 target cases. The intervals are wide.

## Finding: site selection saturates

`held_out_separation` is a correlational score. When the two prompt sets differ in
context, as zero-shot and demonstrated prompts do, they are trivially separable
everywhere and the score stops discriminating between sites. A causal selection rule,
such as the measured effect of each site's removal on a small development set, is the
indicated next step.

## Earlier attempt

A first attempt (`.nrly-lever-qwen-1`, retained locally) stopped at the dial baseline.
No truncation policy was registered, the elaborated outputs hit the 8-token limit, and
those cases were unscored, which made the baseline incomplete. The script now registers
`truncation: "fail"`, and the operation reports unscored cases with that remedy.
